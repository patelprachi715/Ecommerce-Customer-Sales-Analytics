# Business Insights and Recommendations

## Project Summary

This project analyzes online retail transaction data to understand sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, and revenue trends. The analysis was completed using Excel/Google Sheets, MySQL, Python, Tableau, and GitHub.

## Key Performance Indicators

- Total Revenue: 8887208.89
- Total Orders: 18532
- Total Customers: 4338
- Average Order Value: 479.56

## Key Insights

### 1. Country Revenue Performance

The revenue by country analysis identified the top-performing countries. These countries generated the highest sales and represent strong markets for business growth.

### 2. Product Performance

The top product analysis identified the products that generated the most revenue. These products can be prioritized for promotions, inventory planning, and marketing campaigns.

### 3. Customer Revenue Contribution

The customer analysis showed that some customers contributed significantly more revenue than others. This highlights the importance of customer retention and loyalty strategies.

### 4. Monthly Revenue Trend

Monthly revenue analysis helped identify sales trends over time. These trends can support planning for seasonal campaigns, inventory, and promotions.

### 5. Customer Segmentation

Customers were segmented into High Value, Medium Value, and Low Value groups based on total revenue. This segmentation can help the business create targeted marketing strategies.

## Business Recommendations

- Focus marketing efforts on high-value customers to increase repeat purchases.
- Promote top revenue-generating products during strong sales periods.
- Use country-level revenue insights to identify strong markets for growth.
- Review low-performing products and consider pricing, bundling, or promotional strategies.
- Create loyalty campaigns to move medium-value customers into the high-value customer segment.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates an end-to-end data analytics workflow including data cleaning, SQL analysis, Python analysis, Tableau dashboarding, business insights, and GitHub documentation.
